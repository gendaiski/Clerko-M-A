🔒 Scenario 4 · Completion Deferred — for context only

Escrow, transfer & completion

Deferred for this round. Shown for context. Principle: Clerko never holds client money — settlement runs through a regulated third-party escrow/trust provider via API.

1
Deposit into external escrow
Buyer pays the agreed deposit (e.g. 50%) into the regulated trust account; Deal Room shows funding status.
2
Ownership transfer
Legal transfer via Sijilat / MOICT; both parties upload confirmations to the completion checklist.
3
Balance, fee & release
On confirmed completion, the balance is paid, Clerko's success fee is deducted, and net proceeds release to the Seller.
4
Confirm & archive
Both parties confirm the final amount (drives fee & KPIs); the full deal record is archived for both.
